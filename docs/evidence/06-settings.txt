Settings
CAMERA
It runs on your phone and reads your two shoulders and your two hips, and only inside a lesson. From those it measures one thing: how far your trunk leans. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored: that angle exists while the lesson is running and is gone when you leave it. There is no history here to browse, and none to delete.
MICROPHONE — NOT WIRED IN THIS BUILD
No audio permission is requested and nothing you play is captured. The chord and note-attack work the lessons describe was measured on recordings, not in your room. Until an input is wired, every judgement this app makes comes from the camera.
SHARING
Raw video never leaves this device. There is no setting that changes that.
ABOUT THIS BUILD
Three instruments are live: guitar, voice, piano. Every posture reference is built from teaching footage filmed waist-up, which is why nothing below your hips is read. Nothing here has been verified on real hardware.
