CAMERA AND MICROPHONE
Both run on your phone. The camera reads nine upper-body points; the microphone reads level and pitch. Neither stream is uploaded, and neither runs outside a lesson.

Raw video never leaves this device. There is no setting that changes that.
ABOUT THIS BUILD
