SENSORS AND YOUR DATA
What we will ask for.
THE CAMERA
It reads nine upper-body points — hips, shoulders, neck. No face: our teacher references were filmed waist-up, so we have nothing to compare a head against. Ostinato asks for camera access when your first setup check starts, not now, because there is nothing to look at until then.
NOT A MEDICAL TOOL
Ostinato describes how you are sitting compared to a set of teachers. It does not diagnose anything and nothing it says is health advice. If something hurts, that is a question for a person, not an app.
THE MICROPHONE IS NOT WIRED
There is no microphone input in this build. No audio permission is requested and nothing you play is captured. Everything Ostinato judges, it judges from the camera.
YOUR VIDEO
Raw video never leaves this device — there is no setting that changes that. Nothing is stored either: what we measure exists while the lesson runs and is gone when it ends. The camera only runs inside a lesson.
