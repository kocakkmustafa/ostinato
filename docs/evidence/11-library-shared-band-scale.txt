Ostinato
⚙
Measured, not chosen. Correct posture is a band, not a point.
Put your phone where it can see you. The camera reads your two shoulders and your two hips while you practise, and measures one thing: how far your trunk leans. There is no live microphone input in this build — the chord and note work described in the lessons was measured on recordings, not in your room.
YOUR INSTRUMENTS
Guitar
7 guitar teachers · 0.4–6.4° of 24°
Put your phone where it can see you sitting with your guitar. We check your sitting posture against how real teachers sit. This build hears nothing: there is no microphone input, so every judgement comes from the camera.
Voice
6 voice teachers · 0.4–4.4° of 24°
Put your phone where it can see you from the waist up. We check how you hold yourself against six independent singing teachers. This build hears nothing: there is no microphone input, so we watch your posture and never listen to your voice.
Piano
3 piano teachers · 1.9–2.8° of 24°
Put your phone where it can see you at the keyboard from the waist up. We check how you sit against three independent piano teachers. This build hears nothing: there is no microphone input, so your notes are never timed in your room.
The bar on each card is the range those teachers actually occupy, drawn on one shared 0–24° scale so the three can be compared. It is a range, not a target: no point inside it is more correct than another.
